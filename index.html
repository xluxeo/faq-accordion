FAQ

What is Frontend Mentor, and how will it help me?

Frontend Mentor offers realistic coding challenges to help developers improve their frontend coding skills with projects in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It's suitable for all levels and ideal for portfolio building.

Is Frontend Mentor free?

Yes! Frontend Mentor offers a free plan that provides access to a wide range of coding challenges. However, they also offer a paid plan with additional features and benefits for those who want to take their learning to the next level.

Can I use Frontend Mentor projects in my portfolio?

Yes! You can use Frontend Mentor projects in your portfolio. They are designed to be realistic and showcase your skills.

How can I get help if I'm stuck on a challenge?

If you're stuck on a challenge, you can seek help from the Frontend Mentor community. They have forums and discussion boards where you can ask questions and get assistance from other developers.
